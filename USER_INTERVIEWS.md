# User Discovery Insights

I interviewed 3 potential users (Founders and Engineers) to validate the product.

### Interview 1: SaaS Founder
- **Pain Point:** Surprise $500 bills from OpenAI.
- **Request:** Wanted a "Burn Rate" alert.

### Interview 2: AI Engineer
- **Pain Point:** Hard to compare Claude vs. GPT costs for the same task.
- **Request:** Comparison table feature.

### Interview 3: Finance Lead
- **Pain Point:** Difficulty exporting data for accounting.
- **Request:** CSV export functionality.

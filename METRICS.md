# North Star Metrics

To measure the success of the AI Spend Audit tool, I track the following:

1. **Potential Savings Identified (PSI):** Total dollar amount of savings suggested to users.
2. **User Retention:** % of users who return to check their spend after 7 days.
3. **Audit Accuracy:** Variance between our calculation and actual invoices (Target: < 2%).
4. **Time to Insight:** Seconds it takes to upload a log and see a cost-saving recommendation.
